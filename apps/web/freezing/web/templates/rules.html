1. Scoring: Points are heavily weighted to the early miles of each ride.
    • 10 points for each day of 1 mile+.
    • Additional mileage points as follows: Mile 1=10 points; Mile 2=9 pts; Mile 3=8 pts, etc. Miles 10 and over = 1 pt each.
    • LThere is no weekly point cap or distinction between individual and team points. Ride your hearts out.
    • Rides are added for the day, so several short rides count as one long ride. Fractions of a mile count.
    • 0 points for any day you ride less than 1 mile in total.
    Cheat Sheet for the Mathematically Challenged:
    Miles <1 1 2 3 … 8 9 10 11 …
    Points 0 20 29 37 62 64 65 66
  2. Dates: Game runs January 1, 00:00:00 through {{ end_date.strftime("%B %d") }}, 23:59:59. All rides starting from January 1 on count, even though teams may not be assigned for a few days. If you are planning to get a super-early start and be out riding your bike as the year changes, be aware that rides starting before midnight will not be counted by the leaderboard. You need to stop your recording device and restart a new ride after midnight for your miles on January 1 to count. Similarly, you will need to end your last ride before midnight for it to count.
  3. Stationary trainer rides don't count. Take your bike outside and ride it some distance across the surface of the earth in order to earn points. Manual entries will also not be accepted.
  4. Rides made while traveling out of the area do count. On vacation somewhere warm? Lucky you! Find a bike and ride it for some points. Somewhere extra cold? So, so sorry. Find a bike and ride it for some points!
  5. Rides made on e-assist (but not solely electric/motor powered) bikes do count, except that rides on an Elf do not. There was once an inane e-bike debate.
  6. Anyone can award a Pointless Prize for anything. Check out Discord for what people are offering, and feel free to join in and award something yourself. These are typically small fun or cute or symbolic things, not high dollar prizes or anything. But whatever you want! No one will probably argue about you throwing money or new bikes at them if you so desire.

  7. Friends of BAFS are people who would like to participate in events, etc., but not compete as part of a team. In many instances, they are also permitted to participate in Pointless Prizes (although the rules for that are up to each sponsor of a Pointless Prize).

    If you want to be a Friend of BAFS, just follow the normal registration process but when you get to the registration form, stop and don't fill it out. You will be registered as a Friend of BAFS and not assigned to a team.
